Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and the control you keep over it.

1 Who we are

GLORY (“we”, “us”) operates a marketplace that connects creators with brands to price, sign, and settle distribution deals. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our website and app.

2 What we collect

  • Account & identity — name, email, and login, managed by our authentication provider (Clerk), plus your chosen role (creator or brand).
  • Profile & listings — handles, audience figures, rates, portfolio media, and the offers, deals, and messages you create.
  • Payments — processed by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers; we retain deal amounts, payout status, and Stripe identifiers.
  • Media — files you upload are stored with Cloudinary; we keep references and metadata.
  • Usage — device, pages, and basic analytics/log data to keep the service secure and working.

3 How we use it

  • To run the marketplace — matching, offers, escrow, delivery, and payouts.
  • To send transactional email (deal updates, receipts, the waitlist confirmation) via Resend.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • To improve the product. We do not sell your personal information.

4 Subprocessors we rely on

  • Clerk — authentication & account management
  • Supabase — database & storage
  • Stripe — payments & payouts (Stripe Connect)
  • Cloudinary — media hosting
  • Resend — transactional email
  • Vercel — application hosting

5 Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and comply with law (for example, financial records tied to payouts). Delete your account and we remove or anonymise personal data we no longer need.

6 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact us and we’ll act within the timeframe the law requires.

7 Security & transfers

We use reputable providers with encryption in transit and at rest, and least-privilege access on the server. Your data may be processed in countries other than yours; we rely on our providers’ safeguards for those transfers.

8 Children

GLORY is not intended for anyone under 18 (or the age of majority where you live). We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

9 Changes & contact

We’ll post updates here and change the “last updated” date. Questions or requests: contact us.

Terms of Service

The rules of the arena — what you agree to by using GLORY.

1 Acceptance

By creating an account or using GLORY you agree to these Terms. If you’re using GLORY for a company, you confirm you’re authorised to bind it.

2 Accounts & roles

You pick a role — creator or brand — and are responsible for your account, your credentials, and the accuracy of what you publish (rates, audience figures, deliverables).

3 The marketplace

  • A brand sends an offer; a creator accepts or declines.
  • On acceptance, the brand funds escrow through Stripe. Funds are authorised and held, not yet paid out.
  • The creator delivers; the brand releases; the payout is captured to the creator, minus the platform fee.
  • GLORY is the venue — the agreement for the work is between the creator and the brand.

4 Fees & payments

GLORY charges a platform fee on each completed deal, shown before funding. Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe and subject to Stripe’s terms. You’re responsible for your own taxes.

5 Content & intellectual property

Creators keep ownership of the work they produce. You grant GLORY a limited licence to display your profile and portfolio to operate and promote the marketplace. Licences to the brand for the delivered work are set by your deal.

6 Acceptable use

  • No fraud, fake metrics, spam, harassment, or infringing content.
  • No attempts to circumvent escrow, fees, or the platform’s security.
  • Follow the platform rules of any channel where the work is distributed.

7 Disputes, refunds & chargebacks

If a deal goes wrong, work with the other party first. Where escrow hasn’t been released, we may help mediate; refunds and chargebacks follow Stripe’s processes and these Terms.

8 Disclaimers & liability

The service is provided “as is.” To the fullest extent permitted by law, GLORY isn’t liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.

9 Termination

You can close your account anytime. We may suspend or end access for breach of these Terms. Obligations that should survive — payment, IP, liability — do.
